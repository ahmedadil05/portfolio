About

Engineer first, cloud-native by choice

I'm a Software Engineering graduate of Üsküdar University (Istanbul) who spent the run-up to graduation doing real cloud and full-stack work: shipping features as a Full-Stack Developer at Metatip, and building AWS infrastructure for energy-market optimization as a Software Engineering Intern at Flexywatt, where I also authored a 22-page AWS-vs-Snowflake platform evaluation for the team.

My graduation project — an AI-powered network anomaly detection system trained with Isolation Forest on the NSL-KDD dataset — is where the cloud/MLOps direction really clicked. I now work in that stack daily: AWS, Docker, CI/CD, and Python-based ML pipelines, alongside full-stack delivery in Next.js, Flask, and PostgreSQL.

I also lean hard on AI-assisted engineering — Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Gemini CLI, and OpenCode are part of my regular workflow, not a novelty, and I ran a workshop on the space (Cursor, Windsurf, Zed, prompt engineering, a live Codex demo) for other engineers.
